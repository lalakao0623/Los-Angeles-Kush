WORKING DRAFT · TEAM-01

Delivery Intake

Verify the delivery, Metrc manifest, labeling, invoice, and payment terms before accepting stock.

Every incoming delivery

Responsibility
Inventory Team
Estimated Time
5 min – 1 hr · 6 steps
Task
Delivery intake
Version
Working draft v2.0

Before You Begin

  • Have the verified product paperwork and authorized Meadow/Metrc access where needed.
  • Confirm current store naming, pricing, and approval instructions.

Step-By-Step Procedure

  1. 1
    Verify Supplier And Manifest

    Match the distributor, license, destination, manifest number, and expected packages. Escalate an unexpected or mismatched delivery before acceptance.

    Inventory Team
  2. 2
    Count And Inspect The Products

    Compare physical products, package UIDs, quantities, and units of measure with the manifest. Check condition, seals, batch information, and applicable dates; separate discrepancies.

    Inventory Team
  3. 3
    Complete Labeling And Testing Checks

    Use the current DCC checklist for the product type. Check required identity, net contents, cannabinoid information, warnings, universal symbol, batch/lot and manufacturer information where applicable, and testing/COA records. Hold unresolved issues.

    Inventory Team
  4. 4
    Verify Invoice Totals And Terms

    Compare billed quantities, unit costs, credits, totals, and payment terms. Confirm differences with the accounting owner and management before any payout.

    Inventory Team
  5. 5
    Receive Verified Inventory In Metrc

    Use the permitted receiving workflow for the physically verified quantities. Document rejected or held items and confirm the final acceptance record.

    Inventory Team
  6. 6
    Hand Off To POS And Accounting

    Provide the verified invoice and manifest for the Meadow PO and accounting file. Store accepted goods without removing a tag that is still required.

    Inventory Team
When You’re DoneThe task is complete, exceptions are resolved or assigned, and supporting records are filed.

Records To Retain

  • Manifest and verified invoice
  • Receiving notes and discrepancy resolution
  • Metrc acceptance reference

Create A Purchase Order In Meadow

Match received packages, product records, costs, and vendor terms in the POS purchase order.

Each delivery or approved order

Responsibility
Inventory Team
Estimated Time
5–45 min · 5 steps
Task
Purchase order creation
Version
Working draft v2.0

Before You Begin

  • Have the verified product paperwork and authorized Meadow/Metrc access where needed.
  • Confirm current store naming, pricing, and approval instructions.

Step-By-Step Procedure

  1. 1
    Find The Received Transfer

    Confirm Metrc receipt and sync. In Meadow, open Metrc → Incoming Transfers, identify the manifest, and select Create Purchase Order from Transfer from its menu.

    Inventory Team
  2. 2
    Match The Product Records

    Map each package to the correct Meadow product and price option. Resolve missing buttons and incorrect matches before proceeding.

    Inventory Team
  3. 3
    Enter Vendor And Payment Details

    Start the PO and enter the vendor, expected date, payment terms, invoice reference, and relevant notes.

    Inventory Team
  4. 4
    Check Quantities, Costs, And Totals

    Compare imported quantities and entered cost per unit with the verified invoice. Resolve discrepancies rather than changing figures merely to force a match.

    Inventory Team
  5. 5
    Save And Verify The Handoff

    Save the PO, attach the invoice where available, and complete the applicable receiving step in the current Meadow workflow. Verify stock and give the PO reference to accounting.

    Inventory Team
When You’re DoneThe task is complete, exceptions are resolved or assigned, and supporting records are filed.

Records To Retain

  • Meadow PO and invoice references
  • Package mapping
  • Resolved quantity/cost exceptions

Price Option Check

A packaged size and its weight-based amount must represent the actual item. Do not change tracking to bypass an inventory issue. Meadow locks inventory type after stock is received.

Before You Begin

  • Have the verified product paperwork and authorized Meadow/Metrc access where needed.
  • Confirm current store naming, pricing, and approval instructions.

Step-By-Step Procedure

  1. 1
    Search Before Creating

    Search Meadow Products by brand, product, and size. Reuse a correct existing record; avoid duplicate buttons for the same item.

    Inventory Team
  2. 2
    Name The Button Consistently

    Use Brand — Product/Strain — Product Type — Size/Pack Count. Keep spelling and size formats consistent; example: Example Brand — Example Strain — Flower — 3.5g.

    Inventory Team
  3. 3
    Set Brand And Category

    Open Products → Create Product. Select the brand, correct primary category, and applicable subcategory for the POS and integrated menu sites.

    Inventory Team
  4. 4
    Configure Price Option — Type And Amounts

    Match the option to the actual packaged item or flower weight increment. Verify menu unit, amount, package size, retail price, and cannabis content against the item and current Meadow guidance.

    Inventory Team
  5. 5
    Verify Tracking Before Receiving Stock

    Use compliant track-and-trace settings for cannabis and the applicable non-cannabis setting for accessories. Check inventory type before the first stock receipt. Keep unready products inactive.

    Inventory Team
  6. 6
    Review And Release

    Recheck name, category, size, price, package mapping, and connected menu display. Resolve an uncertain configuration with management before activation.

    Inventory Team
When You’re DoneThe task is complete, exceptions are resolved or assigned, and supporting records are filed.

Records To Retain

  • Task record with date and responsible person
  • Supporting references and any approval/exception notes

WORKING DRAFT · TEAM-04

Maintain The Inventory Supplies List

Keep Zebra labels, printer ink, hang tags, price gun stickers, and Kiaro printer supplies available. Email exact item links to upper management for approval and purchasing.

Before supplies run low; review with the team

Responsibility
Inventory Team
Estimated Time
10 min · 6 steps
Task
Inventory supplies
Version
Working draft v2.2

Before You Begin

  • Have the current supplies list, stock counts, printer models, label sizes, and price gun specifications.
  • Use Amazon for Zebra labels, price gun stickers, and regular printer ink; Uline for hang tags; and the designated third-party website for Kiaro supplies. Confirm the Kiaro supplier and upper-management email contact before requesting supplies.

Step-By-Step Procedure

  1. 1
    Check Zebra Labels

    Count remaining Zebra label rolls or sheets. Source replacements from Amazon. Record the compatible label format and size, storage location, quantity needed, and exact Amazon product link.

    Inventory Team
  2. 2
    Check Printer Ink And Kiaro Supplies

    Check stocked printer ink or toner and printer levels. Source regular printer ink from Amazon and record the compatible printer model, cartridge/part number, quantity needed, and exact product link. Review Kiaro printer supplies separately: these come from a third-party supplier website. Confirm the designated supplier website and compatible Kiaro items with upper management, then record each exact item link and quantity needed.

    Inventory Team
  3. 3
    Check Hang Tags

    Count hang tags and attachments; separate damaged or unusable supplies. Source replacements from Uline. Record the tag size/type, Uline item number, quantity needed, and exact Uline product link.

    Inventory Team
  4. 4
    Check Price Gun Stickers

    Count sticker rolls and confirm the size and format fit the store price gun. Source replacements from Amazon. Record the compatible item, quantity needed, and exact Amazon product link.

    Inventory Team
  5. 5
    Email Item Links To Upper Management For Purchasing

    Update the supplies list, then email upper management a supply request for review, approval, and purchasing. Include the exact product-page link for every requested item, including Amazon items, Uline hang tags, and any Kiaro supplies from the designated third-party website. Include each item name, supplier, size/model or part number, quantity, current listed price, and needed-by date. Use the approved work email and management contact. Upper management is responsible for approving the request and placing the order. The inventory team submits the request, retains the email thread and management order confirmation, and coordinates receipt of the supplies.

    Inventory Team
  6. 6
    Receive And Update The List

    Match delivered items and quantities to the request and upper management's order confirmation, confirm compatibility, and report shortages or incorrect supplies. Store accepted supplies in the assigned location and update stock so duplicate requests are avoided. Retain the receipt with the request, approval email, and management order confirmation.

    Inventory Team
When You’re DoneThe task is complete, exceptions are resolved or assigned, and supporting records are filed.

Records To Retain

  • Supplies list covering Zebra labels, printer ink, hang tags, price gun stickers, and Kiaro printer supplies
  • Exact supplier product links and item specifications
  • Request email to upper management, approval thread, and management order confirmation
  • Order receipt and updated stock

Staff And Display Samples Are Different

A display sample is not a staff trade sample. Each uses its applicable authorization, handling, adjustment reason, and supporting records.

Before You Begin

  • Have the verified product paperwork and authorized Meadow/Metrc access where needed.
  • Confirm current store naming, pricing, and approval instructions.

Step-By-Step Procedure

  1. 1
    Review Staff Sample Documentation

    Confirm product, recipient, date, time, package UID, quantity, and authorization. Verify applicable trade-sample eligibility, labeling, packaging, and limits.

    Inventory Team
  2. 2
    Reconcile Authorized Staff Samples

    For a qualifying trade-sample issue, use the Trade Sample adjustment reason. Include employee identity/assigned number, date, and time, and verify the Metrc result.

    Inventory Team
  3. 3
    Reconcile Display Samples

    Match display logs to the correct source package and location. Use Display Sample for the actual allocated quantity and avoid a duplicate direct Metrc adjustment.

    Inventory Team
  4. 4
    Add Or Correct Brands

    Search first. Add an approved brand with consistent spelling, then check associated products and menu presentation.

    Inventory Team
  5. 5
    Add Or Correct Vendors

    Use Inventory → Vendors → New Vendor as needed. Match supplier name, license, and contact information to the invoice; prevent duplicate vendor histories.

    Inventory Team
  6. 6
    Resolve Exceptions

    Compare the resulting Meadow and Metrc records with sample logs. Escalate an unresolved difference to management or Meadow Support rather than forcing a balance.

    Inventory Team
When You’re DoneThe task is complete, exceptions are resolved or assigned, and supporting records are filed.

Records To Retain

  • Staff and display sample logs
  • Reconciliation and sync references
  • Brand/vendor change records

Create RMAs And Email Vendors Or Brands

Send a complete RMA request and track authorization, disposition, and credits.

Each authorized defect-credit or return claim

Before You Begin

  • Have product/package details, supporting records, and the approved hold location.
  • Use the current return, inventory, and waste procedures with authorized system access.

Step-By-Step Procedure

  1. 1
    Create The RMA Record

    Record vendor, brand, original invoice, product, package/batch UID, quantity, defect, photos, requested credit, and hold location.

    Returns & Counts Owner
  2. 2
    Prepare And Send The Email

    Verify the approved contact and send from work email. Include evidence and request written RMA authorization, expected credit, and disposition instructions.

    Returns & Counts Owner
  3. 3
    Hold Goods Pending A Lawful Route

    A brand’s email is not a transport authorization. Confirm the lawful recipient and required manifest/transfer before movement, or follow the approved waste path.

    Returns & Counts Owner
  4. 4
    Track Response And Disposition

    Save the thread, RMA number, follow-up date, authorized pickup/transfer/disposal evidence, and supplier decision.

    Returns & Counts Owner
  5. 5
    Hand Off Credits And Close

    Give confirmed credit details to accounting. Close only when inventory disposition and credit outcome are recorded; escalate denied or stalled claims.

    Returns & Counts Owner

Monthly Count Scheduling

Keep the start-of-month full-store count. Add an interim reconciliation when needed to meet the track-and-trace requirement of at least every 30 calendar days.

Before You Begin

  • Have product/package details, supporting records, and the approved hold location.
  • Use the current return, inventory, and waste procedures with authorized system access.

Step-By-Step Procedure

  1. 1
    Schedule Full-Store Coverage

    Include floor, vault/back stock, delivery locations, promotional stock, samples still on hand, returns, and waste holds. Assign supporting counters and check the prior reconciliation date.

    Returns & Counts Owner
  2. 2
    Control Movements

    Pause stock movement where possible or log every sale, receipt, transfer, and adjustment with its time. Assign clear count-area ownership.

    Returns & Counts Owner
  3. 3
    Count Every Item And Package

    Record actual quantity, unit, package UID, and location, including non-saleable goods. Mark completed areas to prevent omissions or double counts.

    Returns & Counts Owner
  4. 4
    Recount And Investigate

    Compare physical counts with Meadow and Metrc. Review receiving, sales, samples, returns, and waste before proposing corrections.

    Returns & Counts Owner
  5. 5
    Approve Corrections And Escalate

    Give management the evidence for variances. Use authorized adjustments and required loss/diversion/significant-discrepancy reporting; retain investigation records.

    Returns & Counts Owner
  6. 6
    Complete And Schedule The Next Review

    Retain reviewed count/reconciliation results and verify final balances. Set the next deadline so a 31-day month does not create a gap beyond 30 days.

    Returns & Counts Owner
When You’re DoneThe task is complete, exceptions are resolved or assigned, and supporting records are filed.

Records To Retain

  • Count sheets and location coverage
  • Movement log
  • Variance approvals
  • Final reconciliation and next due date

WORKING DRAFT · RET-04

Dispose Of Cannabis Waste Properly

Control waste from identification through disposal using the written waste plan.

Each approved waste/disposal event

Responsibility
Returns & Counts Owner
Estimated Time
30 min – 1 hr · 6 steps
Task
Cannabis waste disposal
Version
Working draft v2.0

Before You Begin

  • Use the approved written cannabis waste plan and authorized disposal route.
  • Prepare source package records, the waste log, and appropriate protective equipment.

Step-By-Step Procedure

  1. 1
    Identify And Classify Waste

    Record source product/package, quantity, and reason. Evaluate hazardous components separately, including batteries, solvents, or contaminated material.

    Returns & Counts Owner
  2. 2
    Secure Pending Disposal

    Use the designated secured receptacle/area with restricted access. Keep waste out of sellable inventory and maintain traceable records.

    Returns & Counts Owner
  3. 3
    Confirm The Authorized Route

    Use a route permitted by the written plan, waste type, and local jurisdiction. Do not assume an ordinary trash route or unverified hauler is acceptable.

    Returns & Counts Owner
  4. 4
    Perform Required Destruction Controls

    Follow applicable rules and the plan. Failed-testing/quality-review batches have additional unusable-material, video-surveillance, and track-and-trace requirements.

    Returns & Counts Owner
  5. 5
    Record Inventory Disposition Once

    Use the authorized Meadow/Metrc workflow for actual quantity/reason. Link the waste log, verify sync, and avoid a second deduction for previously adjusted material.

    Returns & Counts Owner
  6. 6
    Retain Disposal Evidence

    Record date, quantity, staff, method, and hauler/facility. Keep service documentation or required weight tickets/receipts; close the hold after confirmed disposal.

    Returns & Counts Owner
When You’re DoneThe task is complete, exceptions are resolved or assigned, and supporting records are filed.

Records To Retain

  • Waste log and source quantities
  • Approved route/service documentation
  • Required destruction/surveillance references
  • Inventory disposition and disposal receipt
